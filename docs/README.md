# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
OctoAcme follows a structured, customer-first approach to project management that emphasizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. The team uses a repeatable lifecycle from project initiation through planning, execution, release, and retrospective to keep work aligned with business goals and delivery commitments. This documentation hub centralizes the project management processes, roles, workflows, and artifacts used across OctoAcme initiatives.

## Project Management Process Summary
OctoAcme begins each initiative with project initiation, where teams validate the business need, define success metrics, identify stakeholders, and document a lightweight one-pager covering the problem, objective, timeline, risks, and resource needs. After alignment is established, the team moves into planning, breaking work into manageable backlog items with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, and milestones. During execution, the team tracks progress with standups, weekly syncs, project boards, and milestone reviews to remove blockers and keep delivery on schedule. At release time, teams verify that quality gates are met, deploy with a clear rollback plan, and communicate status to stakeholders. After each sprint or milestone, retrospectives capture lessons learned and convert them into action items that improve future delivery.

## Core Principles
- Customer-first: prioritize customer value and usability
- Iterative delivery: deliver small, testable increments
- Clear ownership: each project has named leaders and responsibilities
- Data-informed decisions: track outcomes and adjust based on evidence
- Psychological safety: encourage feedback, learning, and honest risk discussion

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Project Manager (PM)**: coordinates planning, communication, risks, and delivery rhythms
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers**: implement features, write tests, and collaborate on design and review
- **QA / Testing**: validate acceptance criteria and product quality
- **Stakeholders**: provide business context, approvals, and strategic input

## Communication and Governance
OctoAcme relies on regular communication to reduce ambiguity and speed decision-making. Teams use daily standups to monitor execution, weekly PM/PdM syncs to review progress and risks, and milestone demos to validate progress with stakeholders. Risk and dependency management is tracked in a single source of truth, and escalation follows a clear path from the delivery team to the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor when issues have a material business impact. This approach keeps communication structured while leaving room for ad hoc escalations when needed.

## Quality and Assurance Practices
Quality is built into the process rather than treated as an afterthought. OctoAcme expects unit and integration testing where appropriate, smoke tests for critical user flows, CI-based security checks, and strong review practices including small, reviewable pull requests with issue references and acceptance criteria. Releases require passing tests, verification against acceptance criteria, and defined rollback or mitigation procedures for production changes. With these checks in place, the team reduces risk while maintaining a healthy balance between speed and reliability.
