# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads ensure that product changes meet quality standards, acceptance criteria, and release readiness before work reaches production.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy and quality gates for each milestone or release
- Validate acceptance criteria with product and engineering partners
- Coordinate manual and automated testing coverage
- Triage defects, prioritize fixes, and confirm regression risks are closed
- Partner with developers and PMs to support release readiness decisions

### Goals
- Prevent avoidable defects from reaching customers
- Increase confidence in release quality and business readiness
- Improve test coverage and feedback loops throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA readiness reviews
- Defect triage and release sign-off meetings
- Quality dashboards and release checklists

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate builds, reproduce defects, and verify fixes
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are met before launch
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing quality risks and release blockers early

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design clarity, and architectural oversight so that solutions remain maintainable, scalable, and aligned with platform standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical design and implementation approaches
- Define or guide architecture decisions and technical standards
- Identify technical debt, dependencies, and integration risks
- Mentor developers and help resolve complex technical blockers
- Support planning by evaluating trade-offs around cost, scalability, and delivery speed

### Goals
- Maintain a healthy and coherent technical foundation
- Reduce avoidable complexity and rework
- Enable the team to deliver secure, reliable, and scalable solutions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Code review escalations and dependency planning
- Risk reviews for critical technical issues

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure implementation aligns with the intended design
- Provides Product Managers and Project Managers with technical trade-offs and delivery implications
- Works with Security and DevOps roles to ensure systems are both secure and operationally sound

---

## Stakeholder / Business Owner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Owners represent the line of business, funding authority, or strategic sponsor for a project. They help define success and confirm that work remains aligned with business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business needs, value, and target outcomes
- Approve priorities, scope trade-offs, and milestone decisions
- Provide feedback on delivery progress, usability, and business fit
- Coordinate dependencies across organizational teams and decision-makers
- Support resource alignment and executive communication where needed

### Goals
- Ensure projects create measurable business value
- Keep work aligned to strategic priorities and customer needs
- Reduce ambiguity in decisions that affect scope, timing, or investment

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and milestone reviews
- Weekly or monthly status updates
- Escalation conversations when investment, priority, or scope decisions are needed

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm priorities and success metrics
- Works with Project Managers to align schedule, milestones, and stakeholder communications
- Provides final decision-making support to Developers and QA when scope or release trade-offs arise

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet required protections, privacy expectations, and regulatory obligations throughout planning, build, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security and privacy risk
- Define security requirements, reviews, and control expectations
- Coordinate vulnerability scans, remediation follow-up, and compliance validation
- Support incident response communication and risk escalation when needed
- Ensure release activities comply with internal and external standards

### Goals
- Protect customer data and system integrity
- Reduce risk exposure across the software lifecycle
- Maintain confidence in governance and compliance practices

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and design checkpoints
- Compliance and remediation follow-up
- Incident communications and risk escalation paths

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to review architecture, dependencies, and threat posture
- Coordinates with Project Managers and PMs on release gates and risk mitigation plans
- Supports Stakeholders by clarifying the business impact of security and compliance decisions

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers help ensure the team can build, deploy, monitor, and recover software reliably across environments.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and environment configuration
- Support deployment readiness, monitoring, and operational telemetry
- Manage infrastructure and environment dependencies for development, staging, and production
- Assist with rollback planning, incident response, and recovery procedures
- Improve release automation and operational consistency across projects

### Goals
- Enable fast and dependable delivery
- Reduce deployment risk and operational friction
- Improve service reliability and recovery readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Post-deploy verification and incident support
- Infrastructure and pipeline health updates

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Developers by ensuring the build and deploy process is consistent and reliable
- Works closely with Project Managers and stakeholder groups on release timing and rollback readiness
- Aligns with Security teams on secure deployment standards and environment controls

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads focus on user experience, usability, and accessibility so that the product remains intuitive, inclusive, and valuable for its intended users.

### Responsibilities
- Define user journeys, interaction patterns, and interface expectations
- Conduct or guide user research and usability evaluation
- Review feature implementations for alignment with design standards and accessibility requirements
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams on solution trade-offs
- Ensure the user experience supports business value and adoption goals

### Goals
- Deliver usable, accessible, and engaging experiences
- Reduce confusion and friction in core user workflows
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feature kickoff sessions
- User research and usability findings
- Cross-functional alignment on UX trade-offs and acceptance criteria

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to validate customer problems and opportunity areas
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to translate design into implementation decisions
- Supports QA by defining usability and accessibility checks before release

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles reinforce accountability, communication, and decision-making across the full project lifecycle.
